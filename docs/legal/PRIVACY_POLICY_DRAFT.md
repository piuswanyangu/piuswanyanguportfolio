## 2. Summary

- The Website does not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and does
  not set cookies.
- The enquiry form does not send your details to our server. It prepares a
  message in WhatsApp or your email app, and you decide whether to send it.
- Our hosting and security providers process technical data, such as IP
  addresses, when you visit.
- When you use our services, we collect only the information needed for the
  agreed work.

## 3. Information collected when you visit the Website

3.1 **Technical request data.** Every visit sends standard technical
information to the servers that deliver the Website. This includes your IP
address, the date and time, the page requested, the response status, your
browser and device type (the "user agent"), and the page that linked you
here (the "referrer").

3.2 This information is processed by:

  (a) **Cloudflare, Inc.**, which provides our domain name system (DNS),
      security and content delivery. All Website traffic passes through
      Cloudflare's global network.
  (b) **Our web server**, which records this information in access logs. We
      use these logs to keep the Website secure and working and to
      investigate errors or abuse.

3.3 **Network error reports.** Cloudflare asks your browser to report
failed connections to the Website, so that delivery problems can be
detected. These reports are sent by your browser directly to Cloudflare.

3.4 **Email address protection.** Cloudflare automatically disguises the
email addresses shown on the Website to reduce spam. A small Cloudflare
script restores them in your browser.

3.5 **Your theme preference.** If you choose a colour theme, your choice is
saved in your browser's local storage under the name `afrinex-theme`. It
stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it at any time
through your browser settings.

3.6 **What the Website does not do.** The Website does not set cookies, use
analytics or advertising tools, embed social-media trackers, or load fonts
from third-party servers.

## 4. Information you send us

4.1 **Enquiry form.** The form on our Contact page asks for your name, the
service you need, your preferred contact method and a short description of
your request. Pressing the button does not send this information to our
server. Instead:

  (a) **If you choose WhatsApp**, your browser opens a WhatsApp link
      containing your message text. That information is received by
      WhatsApp, a service of Meta Platforms, when the link opens, even
      before you press send in WhatsApp.
  (b) **If you choose email**, your device's email app opens with the message
      prepared. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself.

4.2 **Messages and emails.** When you contact us by WhatsApp or email, we
receive your phone number or email address, your name as shown in that
service, and the content of your messages and any attachments. Our email
is provided by Google (Gmail), and WhatsApp messages are processed by Meta,
under their own privacy policies. `[D26: confirm WhatsApp account type and
any other tools used to store client communications]`

4.3 **Please do not send** passwords, PINs, ID documents or other sensitive
information in a first message. After reviewing your request, we will tell
you what information is actually needed and how to share it safely.
